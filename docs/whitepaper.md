# Letters: Whitepaper

## Vision

Markets on Solana move faster than anyone can read. Letters turns that noise into a small, legible board: ten tiles, ranked by what actually traded, redrawn while you watch. The goal is a surface a person can scan in one glance and trust to be plain about what it knows and what it does not.

## Mission

- Show public market data without editing, ranking tricks or hidden inputs.
- State every outside fact once, in a register, and stay silent about anything that has not been stated.
- Keep the board honest under failure: when the feed stumbles, keep the last good read and say so.
- Offer data, never advice.

## Roadmap

**Stage I, Alphabet.** Ship the live board, the feed health signal and the register that guards every claim.

**Stage II, Words.** Switch on community links and the address slot as each one is stated.

**Stage III, Sentences.** Give holders filters and views so they can cut the board along their own lines.

**Stage IV, Library.** Publish the source, document the method and hand the parts to builders.
